My OAuth Application

This website provides information about a personal OAuth application used to authorize access to Google Drive through rclone.

Purpose

The application is used solely by its owner to access and manage the owner's Google Drive files through the rclone command-line utility.

The application is not offered as a service to the public and is not intended for use by third parties.

Privacy

This application does not operate a separate service that collects or stores personal information. OAuth authorization is provided by Google and access is used by the application owner for personal Google Drive access.
